Summary

Handstead VR is a single-player VR game. It has no user accounts, no online features, no advertising, no analytics and no in-game purchases. The game does not send any information to us or to anyone else. What it saves stays on your headset or PC: your settlements, campaign progress and settings.

What the game stores on your device

The game keeps these files in its own private storage. None of them contains your name, email, account, location or any device identifier.

Where this data is stored (data storage location): only on your own device.

  • PICO and Meta Quest: in the app's private storage on the headset.
  • PC VR (Windows): in your user profile at %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\MiniTown\. "MiniTown" is the game's internal name, kept for save compatibility.

We do not operate servers for the game, and no game data is stored on our systems.

What the game does not do

  • No network communication. The game code contains no networking. The PICO and Meta Quest versions do not request the Android internet permission.
  • No permissions beyond VR. On Android headsets the game declares only the standard OpenXR permissions that let it talk to the headset's VR runtime. It requests no access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage outside its own folder, or phone.
  • No camera, passthrough or microphone use. The game is fully virtual and never shows or records your surroundings or your voice.
  • No hand tracking, eye tracking or body tracking. The game is played with controllers.
  • No device or advertising identifiers are read by the game, and no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services are included.
  • No accounts, chat, multiplayer or user-generated content sharing.
  • No in-app purchases.

Head and controller tracking

To show the virtual world and respond to your hands, the game reads your headset and controller positions and button presses from your device's VR runtime many times per second. This information is used immediately and is not saved, except for the rare diagnostic snapshot described above. It is never sent to us.

Logs and diagnostics

Like most apps:

  • Headsets: the game writes technical messages to the device's system log, which stays on the headset and can be read with developer tools.
  • PC: the game engine writes local log files in the same user-data folder by default.

Logs contain technical information such as errors, timings and rendering state. They are not uploaded by the game. A developer-only diagnostic mode is off by default.

Your headset, PC, store and VR runtime

Your store and device providers operate their own services under their own privacy policies:

  • Meta (Quest): meta.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Steam and SteamVR (Valve): store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement
  • itch.io: itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy
  • PICO: picoxr.com/global/legal/privacy-policy

These services include purchases, accounts, downloads, system crash reports, screenshots and recordings you make with system tools, and any platform backups. We do not receive information from these services through the game.

Contacting support

Support is by email at blue0orbit@gmail.com.

What we receive: if you email us, we receive what you choose to send: your email address, your message, and any screenshots, logs or save files you attach.

How we use it: only to answer you and to fix problems, which is in our legitimate interest and yours (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We never sell or share it.

Where it is kept: this mailbox is a Google Gmail account, so messages are stored by Google under its own privacy policy. Only the developer reads them.

How long: we delete support emails 12 months after our last message with you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Keeping and deleting your data

Game data stays on your device until you delete it.

  • PICO and Meta Quest: uninstalling the game deletes its saves and settings from the headset. The game turns off Android's app backup, so its data is not included in Android device backups. Platform-level backup features are controlled by the platform.
  • PC: delete the folder %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\MiniTown\. If you back up your PC with your own tools, those backups may also contain copies.

Deleting game data permanently removes your settlements, progress and settings.

To ask us to delete anything you sent to support, write to blue0orbit@gmail.com from the same address. We handle deletion requests within 30 days. We cannot see or remotely delete data that exists only on your device.

Children

Handstead VR is intended for players aged 13 and over. It has no social features and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child under 13 has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

The game itself collects no personal data, so there is nothing about you for us to access, correct or delete through the game. Your saves exist only on your device, and you control them.

If you contact us, the publisher is based in Ireland and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to what you send. You have the right to:

  • access it, or have it corrected or deleted;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • receive a copy of it.

Write to blue0orbit@gmail.com to use any of these rights. You may also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (www.dataprotection.ie) or to the data protection authority where you live.
